## Brand & Style

This design system evolves spatial glassmorphism into a warm, deeply atmospheric luxury executive environment. Inspired by high-end architectural lounges, dark smoked glass, and warm ambient backlighting, the aesthetic shifts away from cold analytical blues toward rich smoked obsidian, warm bronze tones, and radiant honey amber glows.

The design philosophy treats interface components not as static digital cutouts, but as physical panes of precision-milled smoked glass suspended above a warm, dimly illuminated physical canvas. Surfaces absorb and refract diffuse ambient lighting, casting warm bronze undertones, soft specular highlights across upper rims, and luminous reflections onto adjacent elements. 

Key attributes:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep obsidian and charcoal brown backgrounds with soft ambient amber illumination.
- **Surface Quality:** Frosted dark smoked glass with deep backdrop blurs (24px to 32px), subtle warm specular sheen along top edges, and delicate translucent border outlines.
- **Accents:** Radiating honey amber, terracotta, and soft peach primary actions, balanced by crisp mint turquoise and soft neon violet indicators for analytical precision.
- **Tactility:** Extra-smooth contours (24px to 28px card radiuses), floating smoked glass navigation docks, and pill-shaped interactive triggers.

## Colors

The palette operates on the interaction between deep obsidian shadows and radiant amber illumination. Color functions as an optical light emitter filtering through heavy tinted glass rather than flat decorative color.

### Palette Mechanics
- **Canvas & Obsidian Bases:** The foundation is anchored in `#141110` (deep obsidian), layering up into `#1A1512` (smoked charcoal) and `#221C18` (warm bronze substrate). Canvas backgrounds feature wide, feathered radial glows centered behind prominent content cards.
- **Primary & Amber Glows:** The primary visual driver is warm amber (`#E68A38`), lifted by honey highlights (`#FFA959`), soft peach (`#F5A65B`), and rich terracotta (`#FF7A45`). Used for active states, key interactive pills, highlighted stat panels, and focused chart bars.
- **Secondary & Analytical Accents:** High-contrast data elements and positive growth indicators utilize luminous mint turquoise (`#2DD4BF`, `#34D399`). Trajectory lines, secondary cohort segments, and specialty badges incorporate luminous violet (`#A78BFA`).
- **Typography & Neutrals:** Primary legibility is guaranteed via warm soft white (`#FDFBF7`), while secondary labels and metadata take on warm cream sand (`#A89F91`).

### Glass Translucency Tokens
- **Base Glass Surface:** `rgba(26, 21, 18, 0.60)` with backdrop blur 28px.
- **Hero Card Glass (Amber Infused):** `linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(230, 138, 56, 0.28) 0%, rgba(26, 21, 18, 0.70) 100%)`.
- **Dock & Floating Chrome:** `rgba(20, 17, 16, 0.78)` with backdrop blur 32px.
- **Glass Specular Stroke:** Top border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.14)` fading to bottom border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`.

## Typography

The typographic strategy balances geometric presence with clear data legibility.

- **Display & Headings:** Set in Space Grotesk. Headings use slightly compressed letter spacing (`-0.015em` to `-0.025em`) to evoke precision instrumentation.
- **Body & Controls:** Driven by Inter for neutral scanning and screen legibility in dense layouts.
- **Tabular Data & Numbers:** All KPI metrics, financial totals, table columns, and dates strictly enforce OpenType tabular figures (`font-feature-settings: "tnum" on; font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).
- **Eyebrows & Micro-badges:** Uppercase tags, category markers, and status indicators apply uppercase styling with `0.05em` letter spacing for clean hierarchy against dark smoked surfaces.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is arranged around a continuous canvas with a floating left navigation rail and a flexible fluid grid for modular smoked glass cards.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Vertical Navigation Dock:** A slim, floating frosted dock pinned to the left margin (width: 72px desktop), separated by `1.25rem` from the main dashboard workspace.
- **Grid Structure:** 12-column fluid grid system with `1.25rem` (20px) gutters on desktop and `1.75rem` (28px) screen padding. Content cards span deliberate proportional blocks (e.g., 3-column quick metric cards, 6-column charts, 12-column data tables).
- **Tablet Reflow (768px – 1180px):** Transitions to an 8-column layout with `1rem` gutters. Metric cards reflow to 2-column or 4-column pairs. The vertical dock compresses to an ultra-slim 56px strip.
- **Mobile Reflow (< 768px):** Transitions to a single-column stacked view with `0.75rem` gutters and `1rem` margins. The vertical rail transitions into a bottom floating smoked pill navigation dock.
- **Inner Glass Padding:** Cards maintain a generous internal rhythm (`space-lg` or 20px–24px) ensuring metrics, badges, and interactive controls never crowd the curved perimeter edges.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through optical transparency, multi-tier backdrop blurs, and directional warm ambient lighting rather than heavy opaque drop shadows.

### Depth Mechanics

1. **Backdrop Filtration:** 
   - Base Panels: `backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(160%)`.
   - Floating Docks & Modals: `backdrop-filter: blur(32px) saturate(180%)`.

2. **Specular Glass Strokes:**
   - Every glass panel features a subtle multi-stop border gradient: `linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12) 0%, rgba(245, 166, 91, 0.10) 30%, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02) 100%)`. This creates a tactile, illuminated top edge.

3. **Ambient Light Diffusions & Glows:**
   - **Hero Amber Card:** Backed by an ambient radial glow (`box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -12px rgba(230, 138, 56, 0.35), inset 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`).
   - **Standard Smoked Cards:** Supported by soft diffused charcoal-bronze depth (`box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65), inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
   - **Floating Nav Rail & Pills:** Cast an elevated drop shadow with faint amber bounce (`box-shadow: 0 20px 48px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75), 0 0 20px 0 rgba(245, 166, 91, 0.04)`).

## Shapes

The system relies on generously softened geometry to balance the dark smoked glass surface aesthetic.

- **Primary Cards & Large Panels:** Rounded with a continuous `24px` to `28px` corner radius (`rounded-xl`), creating an organic, molded lens appearance.
- **Nested Modules & Secondary Containers:** Use `16px` to `18px` radiuses (`rounded-lg`) to preserve proportional concentric curves within parent cards.
- **Interactive Controls, Search Bars, Badges & Pills:** Strictly fully rounded capsules (`rounded-full` / `9999px`).
- **Active Navigation Indicators:** Circular tokens (`40px` diameter) with illuminated radial backdrops, complementing the pill-shaped dock chassis.

## Components

### 1. Smoked Glass Navigation Dock
- **Chassis:** Vertical floating pill container (`width: 64px`, `border-radius: 9999px`, background: `rgba(20, 17, 16, 0.75)`, border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Brand Anchor:** Top rounded pill button displaying the brand mark inside a warm amber glow base (`#E68A38` gradient background).
- **Navigation Items:** Centered icons rendered in `#A89F91`. Hover state triggers soft white transition.
- **Active Indicator:** Circular illuminated disc (`38px × 38px`, background: `#FDFBF7`, icon color: `#141110`, with an outer diffused glow `0 0 16px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.35)`).
- **Notification Dots:** 6px pill badge in warm amber (`#E68A38`) affixed to icon upper quadrants.

### 2. Metric & KPI Glass Cards
- **Structure:** `24px` border radius, frosted smoked glass fill (`rgba(26, 21, 18, 0.60)`), top edge specular sheen.
- **Hero / Spotlight Variant:** Infused with an amber wash (`rgba(230, 138, 56, 0.22)`) fading into smoked bronze, with a prominent white circular action button in the upper-right corner.
- **Metric Content:** Category label in small uppercase sand (`#A89F91`), primary figure styled with `stat-metric` in `#FDFBF7`.
- **Trend Badges:** Inline frosted pills. Positive delta uses mint fill (`rgba(52, 211, 153, 0.12)`) with mint text (`#34D399`) and miniature trend arrows; negative or neutral delta uses warm bronze/red tinting.

### 3. Action Buttons & Interactive Pills
- **Primary Action (Amber Pill):** Rounded-full capsule filled with warm amber gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #FFA959 0%, #E68A38 100%)`), text `#141110` (weight 600), subtle inset top rim highlight and warm outer glow on hover.
- **Secondary Action (Smoked Pill):** Fully rounded capsule with dark translucent fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`), 1px glass rim border, text `#FDFBF7`.
- **Icon Utility Buttons:** Translucent circular buttons (`32px` to `36px`) equipped with delicate arrow or action glyphs.

### 4. Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Global Search Capsule:** Height 44px, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), filled with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Left-aligned magnifying icon with placeholder text in warm sand `#A89F91`.
- **Trailing Shortcut Pill:** Inset keycaps (`⌘K`) styled with a subtle `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` fill and 10px typography.
- **Focus State:** Border transitions to warm amber sheen (`rgba(230, 138, 56, 0.50)`), accompanied by a soft 8px ambient glow.

### 5. Charts, Gauges & Visualizations
- **Bar Visualizations:** Rounded capsule vertical bars (`border-radius: 9999px`). Default bars styled in smoked translucent ivory (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)`), with active/selected bar illuminated in vivid gradient amber (`#FFA959` to `#E68A38`) with an attached floating metric tooltip.
- **Area & Trajectory Charts:** Smooth cubic curves. Primary series styled in luminous mint (`#2DD4BF`), secondary cohort trajectory styled in neon violet (`#A78BFA`) with subtle translucent area fills fading cleanly to `0%` opacity at the baseline.
- **Radial Gauges & Rings:** Segmented circular tracks using dark bronze tracks (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`), highlighted by mint (`#34D399`) and warm orange-red arcs with rounded line caps.

### 6. Data Tables & Customer Lists
- **Container:** Smoked glass panel with integrated header filter pills (e.g., "All", "VIP", "Loyal", "At risk").
- **Header Row:** Muted uppercase sand labels (`#A89F91`), no heavy separators; columns delimited by spatial padding.
- **List Rows:** Hover state reveals an ultra-subtle warm highlight row wash (`rgba(245, 166, 91, 0.04)`).
- **Segment Tags:** Micro-pills with status dots. VIP tags styled with soft amber (`rgba(230, 138, 56, 0.15)` fill, `#FFA959` text); Loyal tags styled with mint (`rgba(52, 211, 153, 0.15)` fill, `#34D399` text).
- **Progress Sliders:** Miniature horizontal rounded tracks indicating loyalty score with mint fill and rounded endpoints.
